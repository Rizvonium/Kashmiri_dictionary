How the any-spelling search works

Every Roman spelling is reduced to a sound key (cxoont, tsoont and tsūnṭh become tsunt) and a consonant root (koshur and kaeshur become kshr). Kashmiri script matches with or without vowel signs, and letter variants like ٲ and ا, ؠ and ی, ۄ and و count as the same. In the writer, when a word has several dictionary spellings, the one whose meaning fits its neighbours is chosen. Tap a word to see the others.
